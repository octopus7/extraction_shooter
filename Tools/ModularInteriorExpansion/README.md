# Roofless interior expansion — 18 modules

Review: `TunaSweeper/SourceArt/Environment/ModularInteriorExpansion/Review.html`. Source `.blend`, 18 FBXs, the shared ImageGen atlas and measured JSON reports live alongside it. UE content is isolated under `/Game/Environment/ModularInteriorExpansion`, with sample map `Maps/L_ModularInteriorExpansion`. The six base modules were restored only from their three dedicated paths at `c2b70edf`; their saved meshes/map/materials are unchanged. No other branch changes were integrated.

## Assembly contract

The base `Tools/ModularInteriorPreview/README.md` and saved base `.blend` are the SSOT. Source units are meters; UE uses centimeters. +X north, +Y east, +Z up. Every exported object's pivot is local zero, object scale one, and rotation zero. FBX copies mirror Y and reverse winding exactly like the base exporter. Do not apply another scale/rotation on import. The principal grid is 50cm; 20cm wall thickness and narrow finish trims are explicit exceptions.

The sign's front/back UV U coordinates are additionally reflected within the sign quadrant on FBX export, because text otherwise reads backward after UE handedness conversion. The source `.blend` keeps readable Blender typography; the FBX proof reloader undoes this documented compensation. Actual UE camera captures verify the final text direction.

FloorHalf is 100×200×20cm and replaces half of the 200×200 base Floor; its top is Z=0. DrainFloor is a full opaque 200×200 replacement slab. Grating bars/recesses are texture shading, **not actual perforations**. FloorEdge attaches outside a slab at local Y=0; it occupies Y=−5..0cm and Z=−20..0cm. It does not overlay the slab top.

WallHalf is 100×20×300cm, with indoor finish at Y=0 and thickness toward +Y. LowPartition is 200×20×100cm. WallEnd starts at a wall endpoint and extends local +X by 5cm; it caps the end, not the face. The annex's low partition cap scales Z to 1/3; the full-height cap stays unscaled. OutsideCorner's outside convex vertex is its pivot. Its L outline is `(0,0),(120,0),(120,20),(20,20),(20,120),(0,120)` cm. It complements the base InsideCorner. Check the manifest for exact end adapters rather than forcing thickness corrections onto the planning grid.

WindowWall is 200×20×300cm with a real rectangular aperture X=50..150cm, Z=100..250cm. ObservationWindow shares this origin: outer frame X=50.2..149.8, Z=100.2..249.8cm, depth 2..18cm. Its 5cm steel frame has 2mm clearance to the wall aperture. The pane is 2cm thick at Y=9..11cm and uses its own material slot. Glass uses simple alpha translucency, opacity .22, no refraction and no two-sided shading. It is a closed thin box (12 pane triangles), so only outward-facing surfaces are drawn. Collision treats the whole insert as one blocking box; this is intentionally conservative glass collision. The frame and glass remain separate closed mesh components in one static mesh.

DoorFrame reuses the exact base Doorway painted-frame coordinates: X=90..100 and 300..310cm jambs, Z=0..240cm, header X=90..310cm at Z=240..250cm, Y=0..20cm. **Its pivot is the base 400cm doorway's origin**, not its 220cm bounding-box corner. The validator verifies every new frame vertex exists in the original doorway mesh. It is a replacement frame for an unframed opening. Do not overlay it on the integrated base Doorway. The annex demonstrates it using scaled base Wall adapters that stop at the frame's outer boundary and the unchanged base DoorLeaf at 2cm clearance and −68° review pose. No duplicate door leaf or doorway wall is exported.

PipeStraight runs local +X for 200cm with 20cm diameter, 8 radial sides; its center is Y=−10cm and rear touches wall Y=0. PipeElbow has 50cm centerline radius, 4 angular segments, and turns +X toward +Z. Its start socket is `(0,−10,0)` and end socket `(50,−10,50)` cm. PipeEnd is a closed 5cm end piece with the same cross-section. These are solid silhouette shells, not hollow fluid passages. Mating caps are buried at joints. DuctStraight is 200×40×40cm, occupying Y=−40..0cm. DuctCorner joins a +X inlet to a +Z outlet, 50cm overall span with 40cm section. Its open terminal is visually closed geometry. Service meshes have no collision, matching decorative wall equipment rather than obstructing movement with small fixtures.

Vent is a 100×50cm wall plate, 8cm deep; louvers are opaque texture shading. EmergencyLight is a 40×20cm amber wall fixture, 12cm deep, with emissive front/top for overhead visibility. It does not add a real light actor. ZoneSign is 100×50cm, 2cm deep, with exact `ZONE 01` typography. Both use rear Y=0. There are no ceiling pieces, beams, pillars, stairs, railings, damage variants or large machines.

## Surfaces and cost

See `MODEL_TABLE.md` for all measured dimensions and per-module counts. Expansion: **18 meshes / 476 triangles / 20 total material slots / 16 simple boxes**. Combined base+expansion: **24 unique meshes / 636 triangles**. Full sample: **96 placements / 1,556 triangles**, excluding review cameras/lights and the base Blender scale mannequin. These are mesh counts, not FPS or draw-call measurements. No Nanite, LOD chain, subdivisions, bevel modifiers or complex-as-simple collision.

The existing 2048² base atlas and 1024² linear grayscale dirt mask are reused. One new 2048² sRGB service atlas serves the grating, vent and sign using different UV regions. ImageGen created the original; `texture.prompt.txt` preserves the exact prompt. `prepare_typography.ps1` performs only mechanical resizing and the explicitly allowed accurate text overlay, retaining both original and typography output. No additional normal/roughness images are generated.

All ordinary surfaces are Opaque with 2 texture reads: their atlas and the shared dirt mask. Glass and amber use 0 texture reads. The service material mixes the same dirt color/roughness/metallic behavior and CPD layout as the base: 0 strength (.65 default), 1 scale (1 default), 2/3 offsets. Higher scale makes smaller stains. Three new materials: ServiceSurface, Glass, Amber. Most slots reference the existing base materials. UV0 is atlas; authored DirtUV/UV1 has 4m coverage; UE build settings generate lightmap UV2 from UV0 without overwriting DirtUV. Reported source UV-channel counts are distinguished from the generated lightmap destination.

Additional dirt is a shared mask, not broad decals. Strong coverage shows some per-instance discontinuity; default .65 is restrained. Small slots, bolts, slats and wear are baked texture detail. Six wall fixtures have six separate real movable rect lights; two broad softboxes are preview-only illumination, with no supporting geometry. Their lighting/shadow costs are not included in triangle counts. Separate static-mesh actors remain convenient for review; no instancing/batching claim is made.

## Review and reproduction

Run `prepare_typography.ps1`, then Blender 4.5 with `--factory-startup -b --python-exit-code 1 --python Tools/ModularInteriorExpansion/build_expansion.py`. `MIE_SKIP_RENDER=1` skips images for geometry iteration; `MIE_RENDER_FILTER` is an optional comma-separated list of preview names. Run `validate_source.py` in a fresh Blender process. It reloads all 24 FBXs, checks signed bounds, pivots, normals, manifold edges, zero-area geometry/UV triangles, two UV channels, materials, UCX count, assembly positive-volume intersections and service socket joins. Reloaded FBXs replace assembly meshes for the proof render.

Run `build_gallery.py` with Python to regenerate the HTML and model table. UE read-only audits are `run_unreal.ps1 -Mode Assets` and `-Mode Map`; they load assets, write JSON and quit. UE needs access to its normal AppData/Zen caches. The actual player BP camera is checked: 1500cm arm, −88° pitch, 0° yaw, FOV70. Captures are editor camera previews, not a PIE gameplay session. Full-height wall tops/headers and wall-facing equipment remain narrow or occluded at steep top-down angles; see the actual UE captures instead of assuming eye-level signage stays readable.

The one-off import/map/capture code is committed with its generated assets and removed immediately in the following cleanup commit. No startup regeneration hook or project plugin/build dependency is added. The reusable Blender modeling source and read-only validators remain. No game map, Blueprint, game string or save schema is changed, and no remote push is requested.
