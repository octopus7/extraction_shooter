# LabSupplyProps authoring contract

Meters, +X north, +Y east, +Z up. Source mesh centered in XY, bottom Z=0; fronts face -Y. Wall control enclosure pivot at rear bottom center (Y=0; body extends negative Y). No object scale. Workbench and sink deck top Z=.90; their footprint 1.60 x .70. Ceiling, beam and building support assets excluded.

ImageGen concept: `TunaSweeper/SourceArt/Environment/LabSupplyProps/References/ImageGen_Concept12.png`. Inspect before modeling. Shapes match this reference, simplify tiny details into atlas.

Single shared opaque material M_LSP_Surface. UV0 uses 4x4 atlas, tile numbers row-major FROM TOP LEFT: 0 pale painted steel, 1 graphite frame, 2 muted teal, 3 stainless top; 4 cabinet drawer/door front, 5 control gauges, 6 analyzer screen/keypad, 7 vent grille graphics; 8 wood plank, 9 plain cardboard, 10 cardboard shipping face, 11 crate front latches; 12 crate lid, 13 bottle label, 14 rubber, 15 dark basin. Per-tile UV interior .025.. .975; panel graphics on the front only, plain tiles on sides. UV1 DirtUV is planar meters/2. Existing ImageGen T_MI_DirtMask supplies additional dust. Vertex SurfaceParams R metallic / G roughness. No extra texture or per-agent material assets.

Each agent owns ONLY its `Tools/LabSupplyProps/parts/<group>.py`. Provide `assets()` returning list of dicts with `key`, `label`, `dimensions` (expected XYZ meters), `parts`, `collision` list of [lo,hi] meters and `notes`. A part is {type:'box',lo:[x,y,z],hi:[x,y,z],tile:int,face_tiles:{'front':tile,'top':tile}}; front=-Y, back=+Y,left=-X,right=+X,bottom=-Z. Or {type:'cylinder',center:[x,y,z],radius:r,depth:d,axis:'X'/'Y'/'Z',sides:8,tile:int}. Or {type:'prism',polygon:[[a,b],...],depth:[lo,hi],plane:'XY'/'XZ'/'YZ',tile:int,face_tiles:{...}} (polygon CCW, solid extrusion). Shared builder closes/recalculates normals, maps UV by dominant face normal, triangulates, joins parts, writes one FBX and simple collision boxes. Keep disconnected solid pieces valid and avoid unnecessary buried geometry. Geometry modules are importable plain Python and must not execute Blender ops or write shared files. Primary owns common builder, all materials, imports, scenes, reports and commits. Agents may run their own static dimensional audit, no git edits/commits. Final Blender and FBX validation by primary.

Compatibility: rack 1.40x.60x2.00, inner usable >=1.28x.48; tier tops .12,.72,1.32,1.92. Supply crate .60x.40x.40 fits shelf. Box bundle 1.00x.70x.70 fits pallet 1.20x1.00x.15. Tray .45x.30 fits all work surfaces. Reserve actual gaps for sink basin, analyzer intake, rack, pallet forks. Collision boxes must preserve these visible gaps; no single hull across open furniture.
