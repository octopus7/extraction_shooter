# Generated waterfall masks

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool (generate mode, not CLI), 2026-09-25. The attached waterfall screenshot supplied visual context; these are new texture assets, not an edited scene screenshot. PNGs are retained unfiltered as generated (1254 x 1254). Both were requested as seamless grayscale masks, imported with sRGB disabled, mask compression and wrap addressing. UE build settings resize the GPU textures to 1024 x 1024, generate simple-average mipmaps and enable streaming; source PNGs remain unchanged. Resizing, rather than padding, avoids introducing a border into the scrolling masks.

- `T_Waterfall_Flow.png` -> `/Game/FX/Waterfall/T_Waterfall_Flow`, used by `M_Waterfall_Curtain`.
- `T_Waterfall_FoamBreakup.png` -> `/Game/FX/Waterfall/T_Waterfall_FoamBreakup`, used by `M_Waterfall_Foam`.

## Final flow prompt

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless grayscale waterfall flow / foam mask texture for a real-time Unreal Engine vertical waterfall material, NOT a scene render. Create a square 1024x1024 full-bleed texture atlas with ONE continuous texture, no panels. Orthographic perfectly flat view of the interior of a falling sheet of water. Natural broad irregular soft waterfall ribbons and turbulent foamy streaks moving strictly from top to bottom. Large and medium structures dominate: uneven sheets of whitewater merge and split organically into asymmetric broken wisps, soft cloudy aerated patches and occasional thin subsidiary streaks, separated by generous quiet dark gray channels of varying width. About 5 to 8 main flow masses across the width, absolutely not dozens of evenly spaced fine strings. Different widths, variable intensity and interrupted lengths; never evenly spaced, never sinusoidal parallel curves, never woven fabric, yarn, hair, wood grain, zebra stripes, rain lines or spaghetti. Grayscale only: dark charcoal base, medium gray fluid body and soft off-white foam; moderate contrast, smooth readable structure, no black outlined edges. Natural water photographic texture but no frozen crystalline sparkles, no granular noise or tiny dots. Seamless tiling horizontally AND vertically, matching top/bottom and left/right edge structures, uniform overall coverage and density over the whole image for scrolling UV use. No horizon, no rocks, no scenery, no pool, no falling-water silhouette border, no perspective, no shadows from surrounding objects, no text, no labels, no watermark. Entire canvas must be useful texture data with no blank margin.
```

Perfectly top-down orthographic close view of naturally aerated water surface foam. Loose irregular islands and broad wispy smears of soft white and light gray froth drift apart over dark charcoal water, connected in places by ragged lacy tendrils.
