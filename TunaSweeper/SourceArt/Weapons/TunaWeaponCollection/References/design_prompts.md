# Tuna Weapon Collection design prompts

All images were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. The three selected
premium references are the final grounded-near-future revision requested by the
user. Earlier rejected revisions are retained under `Iterations/` for design
traceability and are not modeling targets.

## SMG Standard

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: modeling reference for a stylized Unreal Engine 5 top-down extraction shooter weapon prop
Primary request: design an original compact standard submachine gun, ordinary and practical, clearly readable at game-camera distance
Scene/backdrop: neutral warm-gray studio board
Subject: one unbranded fictional SMG with a conventional straight receiver, short barrel shroud, simple iron sights, compact fixed stock, ribbed polymer pistol grip, and curved box magazine; approximately 58 cm long
Style/medium: polished hand-painted 3D game prop concept, believable industrial product, chunky clean forms, soft bevels, mildly worn but maintained
Composition/framing: large left-facing side elevation plus smaller front three-quarter and rear three-quarter views, consistent proportions, no exploded view, no internal components
Lighting/mood: soft studio lighting, crisp silhouette
Color palette: charcoal parkerized metal, dark olive polymer, subdued tan grip panels, tiny safety-orange indicator only
Materials/textures: matte coated steel, injection-molded polymer, fine grip stippling, restrained edge wear
Constraints: fictional game visual asset only; muzzle points to image right in every view; forms must be straightforward to recreate in Blender; no hands, character, ammunition, labels, dimension callouts, text, logos, watermark
Avoid: real-world brand identity, photoreal manufacturing blueprint, exposed firing mechanism, excessive greebles, neon sci-fi glow, toy-like proportions
```
