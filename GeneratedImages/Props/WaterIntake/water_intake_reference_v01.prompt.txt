Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: 3D modeling reference concept sheet for a water intake facility in the TunaSweeper game demo, to be subsequently built with Blender Python.
Primary request: Design ONE coherent compact, charming, slightly weathered industrial stream-side water intake pump installation, with a highly readable silhouette for an elevated top-down action game camera. This is a first concept proposal, not an engineering drawing.
Required functional parts: a horizontal cylindrical coarse intake screen basket with widely spaced sturdy bars and circular end rings, dipping partly into a shallow stream; a thick continuous intake pipe connecting the basket to a compact centrifugal pump and cylindrical electric motor on a low concrete plinth on the bank; a discharge pipe leaving the installation toward an underground bunker; a large reachable manual valve with replaceable wheel handle on the dry bank; one simple small control cabinet. The intake screen must be clearly visible and accessible for crowbar cleaning. Leaves, tangled aquatic plants and a few branches are a separate removable clump caught against the intake basket, not integrated into the permanent metal mesh. The pump, screen frame and cabinet remain in place after cleaning. The broken valve initially has a short exposed stem and broken handle remnant; the replacement is a complete simple wheel, distinct and easy to model.
Composition: landscape 3:2 professional concept sheet on warm light neutral background. Left two-thirds: a large clean elevated three-quarter view of the complete facility, small cutaway patch of grassy bank and shallow water only to clarify installation; no surrounding landscape. Right one-third: three clean isolated detail studies stacked vertically: clean cylindrical screen, removable weeds-and-branches clump, and broken valve stem alongside intact replacement wheel. Detail studies must match the main design exactly. Generous negative space and restrained fine dividing lines. No decorative UI or captions, no typography, no humans.
Style: appealing stylized game prop 3D concept render, chunky bevelled simple forms, believable functional construction, clean broad color regions, subtle soft shading, rounded industrial design, low-to-mid-poly achievable geometry. Moderate wear limited to a few large patches, no gritty microtexture or noisy speckling. Muted painted industrial metal and concrete with a restrained contrasting warm valve wheel accent and simple indicator lens. Bright soft studio-like daylight with readable surfaces and soft ambient shadows.
Modelability: limited component count, repeatable cylindrical pipes, elbows, flange rings with few large bolts, simple motor fins, sturdy screen bars, solid cabinet. Clear joints, no floating parts, no dense tiny pipe networks, no inaccessible hidden mechanism. Facility roughly a few meters across, no giant factory or building, no roof occluding the interaction points, no decorative tanks, no steampunk, no science fiction reactor, no photorealistic grime. Prioritize cylindrical intake screen and repairable valve as the visual story.
