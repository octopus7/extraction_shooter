Prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one polished visual target image for a stylized top-down action game's lava ground area-of-effect, caused by a fire projectile impact. Single coherent hero scene, no panels, no text, no UI. Camera: elevated three-quarter top-down view, about 60 degrees downward, full circular footprint visible with generous margins. On a simple dark desaturated slate ground plane is an irregular roughly circular 4-meter-wide patch of molten ground. The ground has fractured into 12-18 large chunky angular charcoal-black basalt crust plates, slightly lifted and tilted but predominantly low and flat, separated by broad connected rivers of vivid molten orange lava. Center contains a small brighter yellow-orange molten impact pool, with major glowing fissures radiating outward and branching into a connected network. Black crust occupies about 60 percent of the area, visible lava channels 40 percent; strong readable shapes even at game camera distance. Outer edge jagged and naturally broken, black-red cooling crust blending onto normal floor; a few cracks extend beyond the main footprint. Sparse short curling pointed orange flames lick upward from only a few fissures, some small molten bubbles, a few airborne glowing droplets and black rock fragments, and restrained drifting embers. The lava surface remains clearly visible; flames must not obscure it. Warm emitted light gently spills onto nearby ground. Style: premium stylized 3D game VFX look-development render, hand-painted color design, broad clean faceted forms, smooth lava with large flowing yellow-orange streaks, crisp silhouettes, very restrained bloom preserving orange saturation. No realistic gritty grain, no micro speckles, no photoreal volcanic scenery, no tall rocks or volcano mound, no huge explosion or fire column, no smoke curtain, no characters, no magical runes or decorative circles. The result should look like a practical buildable game effect resting on the ground, hot, weighty, and visually rich while cleanly readable.
